Privacy Policy

Meeting Backup · Effective October 23, 2026

Operator and purpose

Meeting Backup is operated by the owner of the GitHub account boyoung2929 for personal meeting-recording backup, transcription, and preparation of summaries and slides.

Data accessed and used

The application accesses authorized Google Drive file metadata and recording contents needed for its configured workflow. It stores downloaded recordings, transcripts, summaries, slides, and processing metadata. Google OAuth credentials are used to maintain the authorized connection.

Storage and processing

Recordings are downloaded to storage managed by the operator. Speech transcription currently runs locally on the operator’s server. Transcript text is sent to configured OpenAI/Codex services for AI-assisted summarization and slide preparation. The provider’s applicable service terms and data controls govern that processing. Generated outputs may be uploaded to designated Google Drive folders.

Sharing and providers

Recordings and transcripts are not published on this informational website. Access to Drive files follows the sharing settings chosen by the owner. Google provides Drive and authorization services; OpenAI provides the configured summarization services. Meeting Backup does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. The operator does not use Google user data to train general-purpose AI models. Meeting Backup’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Downloaded recordings and generated outputs are retained until the operator deletes them. Revoking Google access stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete previously downloaded files, local copies, generated outputs, or backups. Contact the operator to request deletion of those copies; provider-side retention is governed by the applicable provider policies.

Your choices and security

The Google account owner may revoke the application’s access in Google Account settings. OAuth credentials are stored in access-restricted server configuration and are not included in this public website. The operator manages access to recordings and generated outputs.

This website

This static website contains no recording-upload form, application login, advertising, or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process standard technical request information under GitHub’s own privacy policy.

Contact and updates

Questions and data requests: boyoung2929@gmail.com. Changes to these practices will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
