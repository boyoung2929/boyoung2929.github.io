Meeting Backup

Meeting Backup is a personal application operated by the owner of the GitHub account boyoung2929. It helps the owner and invited collaborators back up meeting recordings, create transcripts, and organize summaries and presentation slides.

How it works

  1. The owner authorizes access to Google Drive. Recordings are placed in the designated meeting backup folder.
  2. The owner’s server downloads recordings and generates transcripts locally.
  3. Configured summarization tools process transcripts to create meeting summaries, action items, and slides. Generated files may be saved to designated Google Drive folders.

Why Google Drive access is requested

Read access is used to find and download recordings from the configured folder. The server’s current recording-download connection requests read-only Drive access. Separate app connections may upload recordings or generated outputs when authorized by the owner. Granted permissions may cover more files than the configured folder; the download workflow is configured to use that folder.
