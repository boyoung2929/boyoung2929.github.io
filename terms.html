Meeting Backup is a personal tool operated by the owner of the GitHub account boyoung2929. It is intended for the owner and invited collaborators to back up authorized recordings and generate transcripts, summaries, action items, and slides.

Transcription and AI-generated summaries can contain errors, omissions, or incorrect interpretations. Review results against the original recording before relying on them. The application does not guarantee continuous availability or successful backup; retain necessary original copies.
